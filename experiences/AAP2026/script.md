# Script — AAP2026

Generation prompts for AI video. Each shot is 5–8 seconds, so paste one shot at a time into the generator. Paste the MASTER STYLE block with every shot.

Text, numbers and the end card are NOT generated. Add them in CapCut or hyperframes.

---

## MASTER STYLE (paste with every shot)
```
Vertical 9:16, 1080x1920. Cinematic documentary look, natural warm light,
soft pink-and-white colour grade. Handheld-feel camera, shallow depth of
field, realistic faces and skin tones, no cartoon or CGI look. Same young
Malaysian Orang Asli woman in every shot (late teens, shoulder-length dark
hair, simple cotton top) and the same village setting (stilt house, rainforest
edge). No readable text, logos or brand names in frame. No music.
```

---

## SEGMENT 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15)

**1A (0:00–0:07)**
```
Close-up. A young Orang Asli woman sits on a wooden step of a stilt house at
dawn, phone in hand, its screen glowing faintly on her face. She looks up,
thoughtful. Rainforest edge behind her. Slow push-in, 35mm lens.
```

**1B (0:07–0:15)**
```
Medium close-up. She lowers the phone slightly and looks straight into the
lens, calm. Slow push-in continues. Mist drifts behind her.
```

- 5→6: slow fade to white, then soft pink
